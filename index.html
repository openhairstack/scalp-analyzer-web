Ethical & Clinical Boundary Disclaimers

Scientific Demo: Open Hair Stack provides open-weights neural computer vision models for trichology and dermatological research. Code, data, and model graphs are fully auditable in the open repository.

Non-Diagnostic: This software is not an FDA/CE cleared medical device, diagnostic test, or clinical treatment plan. Follicular counts and gradient evaluations must not substitute for physical evaluation by a board-certified dermatologist.

Data Privacy Guarantee: All ONNX graph operations, letterboxing, bounding box decoding, and canvas rendering happen entirely within your local browser's WebAssembly sandbox. No pixels or patient metadata ever leave this machine.

Baseline Model Notice: v0.1 was trained on ~688 open CC-BY trichoscopy micrographs. Community feedback and edge cases help advance future foundation checkpoints: report findings on GitHub.
